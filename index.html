Required Watching

  • Glee (2009-2015)
    This show is so horrible yet so beautiful. You get invested in absolute stupidity. It is wonderful. It kills your brain yet it grows full of love for Sue Sylvester.
  • Community (2009-2015)
    This show is Blorp Smoop's personal favorite show. It is very good and emotional and relatable. Abed Nadir is Blorp Smoop's favorite character ever.
  • The Big Bang Theory (2007-2019)
    Blorp Smoop grew up with this show. Blorp Smoop is very emotionally attached to this show and these characters. Very much a period piece. But so peak.
    • Young Sheldon (2017-2024)
    Best prequel show ever. Insanely well-written. Super duper rec. Blorp Smoop has watched this entire show 7 times MINIMUM.
